# Gannon Waye Music Content Workflow

## Safe production flow

1. Draft idea in Pressmaster, Codex, or Base44 admin.
2. Check the idea against Gannon Waye Music voice and current release priorities.
3. Create a short script, caption, or post.
4. Attach approved media only: artwork, moving cover MP4, exact family photos, or approved Gannon footage.
5. Export the correct format:
   - TikTok/Reels/Shorts: 9:16, 1080×1920, MP4, burned captions.
   - Instagram feed: 4:5, 1080×1350, MP4 or image.
   - Website: compressed MP4 or image with poster thumbnail.
6. Store final assets in the approved media library or Google Drive.
7. Add to the content calendar.
8. Wait for Gannon approval.
9. Publish or schedule only after approval.

## Current release messaging guardrails

- “Without You Here” was written in Gannon’s loungeroom, not in a garden.
- Mum’s Garden is the memorial destination: a place to visit Sonia, leave love, listen to the song, and journey through her life.
- Do not write as if Sonia is literally alive inside the site.
- Do not over-sell merch inside memorial content. Keepsakes can appear gently and secondarily.

## Immediate reusable script shape

Hook → one honest sentence → one emotional memory → one invitation → song/page call to action.

Example:

> I wrote “Without You Here” in my loungeroom, but it became a garden in my heart. This song is for my mum, Sonia. I still want to tell her things. I still reach for her advice. I still carry her with me. So I’m building Mum’s Garden as a place people can visit, remember her, and leave love.
