## 📖 BIOGRAPHY — LONG VERSION (500 WORDS)

Gannon Waye is an independent Australian singer-songwriter based in Maidstone, Victoria, whose music exists at the intersection of emotional vulnerability and melodic clarity. He does not write to chase radio trends; he writes because, in his own words, "if I don't, the feeling stays." His instruments are a notebook on the lounge-room floor, raw lyrics, and lived truth — converting private suffering into songcraft that others can recognise as their own.

Gannon's journey began in Adelaide, where he sang from a young age — runner-up in Adelaide Search for a Star, Top 100 in the early days of Australian Idol — but the past decade has been about something far more personal than trophies: developing his own voice and writing from lived experience. Now calling Melbourne home for over 13 years, his perspective is shaped by travel, culture, and a deep care for people's wellbeing, sometimes more than they realise about themselves.

That story has not been simple. Gannon has navigated childhood bullying and the isolation of feeling different; the Adelaide and Melbourne gay scene and the contrast between bright party lights and 3 AM emptiness; faith, church, and the wall of religious judgment that taught him grace was never conditional; and relationship abuse, account sabotage, and starting over from nothing. Through all of it, he learned that being misunderstood doesn't mean you're wrong — it often means you're seeing something others aren't ready for yet.

The turning point of his art, and his life, arrived on 27 April 2022, when his mother, Sonia Katisa Waye (5 February 1961 – 27 April 2022), passed away. Twenty days after a stage-four diagnosis, she took her last breath holding his hand. In the early hours of Mother's Day 2026, Gannon sat on the loungeroom floor and wrote "Without You Here" — an acoustic letter to the voice he still reaches for, the wisdom he still misses, and the love that never left him, even after she did. The line at its centre — *"your last breath took mine away, there's not much more I have to say"* — carries the entire weight of the song.

"Without You Here" follows his debut single "Thank You", a self-respect anthem about seeing a painful relationship clearly, breaking a cycle, and choosing not to return to it. Together, the two singles map the emotional arc of his forthcoming 15-track debut album, *I'm Still Here*, slated for release on 10 March 2027, produced with Victorian producer Will Henderson (Willing Music). The album chronicles an extraordinary personal journey — grief and survival, breakthrough and boundaries, freedom and rebirth.

Gannon has pledged 10% of all music support to 1800RESPECT, Australia's national domestic, family, and sexual violence counselling service, turning private survival into a public sanctuary. At gannonwaye.com, listeners can stream the music, explore the interactive memorial garden dedicated to Sonia, and support the project directly. Gannon Waye is building a lasting legacy of authentic Australian songwriting — for anyone sitting alone in the dark, needing to know they are not crazy and that they can keep going.

### 2. Family Architecture

**Key People:** Sonia (Mum) — the central sanctuary. Pa — the quiet protector. Dad — the complex relationship of tension and distance. Three siblings: Carla, Jarrad, Crystal.

### 3. School Rejection & Childhood Bullying
**Narrative:** Experiencing the deep isolation of feeling different as a child. Schoolyard bullying forced him to build internal armour early, and music became his private sanctuary where no one could interrupt or reject him.

### 4. The Gay Scene & Searching for Belonging
**Narrative:** Stepping into the Adelaide and Melbourne gay scene seeking acceptance after childhood isolation. Navigating the contrast between bright party lights and 3 AM emotional emptiness — learning that wearing a mask of happiness couldn't heal unhealed wounds.

### 5. Faith, Church & Grace vs. Judgment
**Narrative:** Seeking structure and spiritual redemption in church, only to hit the wall of religious judgment. He realised grace wasn't conditional, and stopped asking an institution for permission to exist.

### 6. Relationship Abuse, Sabotage & Account Loss
- *"Recently my ex had my Instagram and Facebooks disabled and I cannot get them back. Now I am starting from nothing. This is not good."*
- *"Choosing self-respect over repetition."*

**Narrative:** Navigating relationship trauma and watching his ex compromise his social accounts. Refusing quiet compliance, Gannon wrote "Thank You" as a declaration of self-respect and built his unshakeable home base at gannonwaye.com.

### 7. Sonia (Mum) & The Living Garden (27 April 2022)

**Narrative:** Preserving Mum's Adelaide backyard — monstera, elephant ears, orange vines, concrete table, burgundy robe, morning coffee, Onya & Gay's Archway — in an 8-scene interactive sanctuary at gannonwaye.com.
