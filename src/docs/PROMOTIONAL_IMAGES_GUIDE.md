## 🎨 EDITING TIPS

### Quick Edits in Canva:
1. **Add text overlays** - Use fonts: Playfair Display (headings), Inter (body)
2. **Color matching** - Use brand colors: Gold (#D4AF37), Black (#0A0A0A)
3. **Resize for platforms** - Canva's Magic Resize tool
4. **Add your logo** - Subtle watermark in corner

### Advanced Edits:
- **Remove backgrounds** - Use remove.bg for product isolation
- **Add motion** - Import to CapCut for animated posts
- **Create carousels** - Split images into multiple slides
