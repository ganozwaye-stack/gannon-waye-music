## Proposed album order: emotional story version

This order is designed to feel like a complete emotional arc rather than just a pile of singles.

### 1. Thank You

Purpose: opens the era with power, clarity, and identity. It says who Gannon Waye is from the first track.

### 2. I've Been Set Free

Purpose: continues the escape and survivor energy. It keeps momentum after Thank You and shows the boundary has become permanent.

### 3. You're So Arrogant

Purpose: sharp, direct, character driven. It gives attitude and bite after the opening survivor sequence.

### 4. Will You Even Listen

Purpose: shifts from confrontation into emotional exhaustion. It asks the question that sits underneath the conflict.

### 5. One Day

Purpose: gives hope and long range healing. This should feel like the first breath after the storm.

### 6. Set Free

Purpose: spiritual and emotional release. This can act as the midpoint reset.

### 7. Without You Here

Purpose: the emotional heart of the album. This is the Mum tribute and should not be buried too early.

### 8. This Is Me

Purpose: identity reclamation after grief. The album turns from survival into becoming.

### 9. To You vs To Them

Purpose: truth telling and audience connection. Could be spoken, sung, or hybrid depending on final production.

### 10. Final track: Carrying Your Love With Me

Purpose: closing track concept based on the final emotional message from Without You Here. If this does not exist yet, it can be written as the album closer.
