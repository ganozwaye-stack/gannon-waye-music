HeyGen does not always respond like a film actor, so give simple emotional directions.

Use:

- "speak slowly and honestly"
- "small pauses after emotional sentences"
- "soft eyes"
- "controlled breath"
- "slight smile when remembering love"
- "voice catches slightly, but stay composed"

- "cry dramatically"
- "overjoyed influencer energy"
- "corporate presenter"

## Series architecture

### Episode 1: This Is Me

Core idea: Gannon introduces the whole world honestly.

Recommended setting: studio / mic.

Draft 3-minute script:

```text
Hey, I'm Gannon Waye.

And if you're finding me for the first time, I don't really want to introduce myself like a brand. I want to introduce myself like a person.

I make music because I had to find somewhere to put the things I couldn't keep carrying in silence.

For me, songwriting became a way of journaling. A way of surviving. A way of sitting with grief, isolation, faith, anger, love, and all the parts of life that don't fit neatly into a caption.

I don't want my music to feel like something made for fame. I want it to feel like something you can borrow when you need strength.

When I released Thankyou, it wasn't just a song. It was an anthem for anyone who had lost their strength, their independence, or their sense of self, and needed to stand back up.

And with Without You Here, it's even more personal.

That song came from missing Mum. It came from the room I was sitting in, from the ache of realising that love doesn't end just because someone is not physically here.

That's why I'm building Mum's Garden. Not as a perfect polished memorial, but as a place to come and spend time with her. To remember her. To learn about her. To let the song and the page hold some of the love that still has nowhere to go.

This is the world I'm building now.

Music. Story. Memory. Healing. Resilience. A place for people who have had to keep going when life didn't give them a gentle option.

And yeah, I want it to grow. I want the songs to reach people. I want the merch, the coaching, the page, the community, all of it to work.

But underneath all of that, the heart is simple.

I know what it's like to feel alone.

And if anything I make helps someone feel less alone for even three minutes, then it matters.

So this is me.

Not perfect. Not polished into something fake.

Just honest.

And still building.
```

Performance notes:

- Opening: steady, direct, no smile yet.
- "I make music..." soft eyes, slower pace.
- "Without You Here..." pause before and after.
- "Mum's Garden..." slight warmth, almost a smile, not sad-only.
- Final "still building" calm confidence.

### Episode 2: The Room Where I Wrote It

Core idea: correct the story - the song was written in the loungeroom, while Mum's Garden is the tribute destination.

Recommended setting: loungeroom.

Draft 3-minute script:

```text
I want to tell the truth about Without You Here.

I didn't write it standing in some beautiful garden with everything looking poetic and peaceful.

I wrote it in my loungeroom.

That matters to me, because grief doesn't usually arrive with perfect lighting. It arrives in ordinary rooms. It sits with you when the house is quiet. It finds you when you're trying to do normal things and suddenly the missing hits you again.

Without You Here came from that place.

It came from sitting with the reality that Mum wasn't walking through the door. That I couldn't just call her. That there were things I still wanted to say, and moments I still wanted her in.

The garden came later.

Mum's Garden is the place I'm building around the song. A place for people to come and spend time with her. To see her photos. To hear about who she was. To leave memories. To sit with the love instead of only the loss.

Because I don't want the page to feel like a grave.

I want it to feel like presence.

Like walking from the sky down into a garden. Like being reminded that someone can be gone from the room but still everywhere in your life.

That is what Without You Here is about for me.

Not moving on.

Not forgetting.

Not pretending it doesn't hurt.

It's about keeping love alive in the only ways we can.

Through memory.
Through music.
Through words.
Through the people who still carry her.

So when you hear the song, and when you visit the page, I hope you don't just see my story.

I hope it gives you space for your own.
```

Performance notes:

- "I wrote it in my loungeroom" should be plain, grounded, almost conversational.
- Slight smile at "The garden came later" because this clarifies the creative world.
- Avoid over-sad delivery; the episode should feel human and truthful.

### Episode 3: Mum's Garden

Core idea: invite people into the memorial page.

Recommended setting: garden / sky.

Draft 3-minute script:

```text
Mum's Garden is for Sonia.

It's a tribute page, but I don't want it to feel cold or distant.

I want it to feel like somewhere you can visit.

Somewhere you can scroll from the sky into the garden, see her face, feel her humour, learn who she was, and understand why she meant so much to us.

For people who knew her, I want it to feel familiar.

For people who never got to meet her, I want it to feel like they still get a sense of her.

Her warmth.
Her strength.
Her cheek.
Her love.
Her way of making people feel held.

This page is connected to Without You Here because the song came from missing her.

But the page is not only about loss.

It's about love that still moves.

It's about family photos, memories, music, stories, and a place where people can leave their own words for her.

If you have a memory of Mum, you can share it through the page.

If you didn't know her, you can still come and sit with the story.

Because sometimes someone else's grief helps you understand your own.

And sometimes a song gives you permission to miss someone properly.

That's what I'm hoping this becomes.

A place to remember.
A place to listen.
A place to spend time with Mum.
```

Performance notes:

- Warm, reverent, never spooky.
- "Her warmth. Her strength..." slightly brighter rhythm.
- CTA should be soft, not salesy.

### Episode 4: Thankyou and Taking Strength Back

Core idea: connect Thankyou to resilience/self-respect.

Recommended setting: street/training/resilience or studio.

Draft 3-minute script:

```text
Thankyou was the first time I really felt like I could turn pain into a declaration.

Not a perfect one.

Not a polished motivational quote.

Something real.

It was about the moment you realise you have given too much of yourself away. The moment you realise your strength, your independence, your self-respect, they have to come back to you.

For me, music has always been a place to tell the truth without asking permission.

And Thankyou became an anthem for that.

For anyone who has had to stand there and say, I might be hurt, but I'm not finished.

I might be tired, but I'm still here.

I might have lost myself for a while, but I can come back.

That's the kind of music I want to make.

Not music that pretends life is easy.

Music that gives you something to hold when it isn't.

And that's also why the work is expanding into coaching and resilience.

Because the songs are one part of it.

But the bigger mission is helping people rebuild the part of themselves they thought life had taken.

If Thankyou found you at the right time, I hope it reminded you of something.

You are allowed to come back stronger.

You are allowed to choose yourself again.

You are allowed to stop apologising for surviving.
```

Performance notes:

- Stronger voice than Mum episodes.
- More direct eye contact.
- No crying here; this is controlled strength.

### Episode 5: The Business Of My Life

Core idea: explain the ecosystem without sounding corporate.

Recommended setting: desk / command centre.

Draft 3-minute script:

```text
What I'm building now is bigger than one song.

But it still starts with the same heart.

The music is the doorway.

The stories explain the music.

The website holds the world around it.

The merch gives people something physical to carry.

The coaching side is about resilience, mindset, and helping people rebuild without making them feel like they're broken.

And the AI systems, the dashboards, the content tools, all of that, they are not there to replace the human part.

They're there to help me stay consistent enough to actually get the work out into the world.

Because I know what it's like to have a lot inside you and no clean system to move it through.

So I'm building one.

A place where ideas become scripts.
Scripts become videos.
Videos become posts.
Posts lead people back to the music, the page, the store, the community, and the work.

But the rule is simple.

Nothing should become fake just because it becomes efficient.

I don't want automation that loses the heart.

I want a system that protects the heart.

That's the business of my life.

Turning the things I've survived into something useful.

Turning grief into memory.
Music into connection.
Resilience into service.
And ideas into something real enough to help people.
```

Performance notes:

- Calm but more strategic.
- Slight smile on "I'm building one."
- Confident final beat.
