## Why Laptop Stand Is Next

The laptop stand has the best mix of:

- Clear pain point: neck pain, bad posture, creator/remote-work setup.
- Higher perceived value than small cable accessories.
- Low return risk in the source record.
- Hero product potential marked true.
- Clean content angle: desk setup transformation, posture reset, before/after workstation.

The risk is competition. Laptop stands are common, so the listing cannot be generic. It needs stronger positioning around creator desks, home-office comfort, and visual setup upgrade.
