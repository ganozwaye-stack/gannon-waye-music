# Security and Approval Rules

## Never commit secrets

Do not commit API keys, passwords, OAuth tokens, cookies, private keys, or session data. Use platform secrets, GitHub secrets, Base44 secrets, or local `.env.local` files excluded from git.

## Public action approvals

Ask Gannon for final approval before:

- Publishing or scheduling posts.
- Sending emails.
- Deploying live changes.
- Changing domains, DNS, billing, Stripe, Shopify, pricing, or payment settings.
- Connecting new public platforms.
- Publishing family memories or memorial photos.
- Deleting or trashing any files/data.

## Memorial consent

Submissions to Sonia’s page must remain private by default. A submission can only appear publicly when:

1. The submitter consented to review.
2. The submitter consented to possible publication.
3. Gannon approved the submission.
4. The image/text fits the memorial tone and privacy rules.

## AI voice and avatar safety

- Gannon’s AI twin requires explicit consent and approval.
- Sonia’s original audio may be labelled and played as original audio.
- Do not generate new Sonia speech or imply an AI system is Sonia herself.
## 2026-07-10 external avatar/media rule

- Do not upload Sonia photos, videos, service material, or voice notes to HeyGen or any external avatar tool without Gannon approving the exact asset package first.
- Do not publish or embed any generated Sonia-style avatar/video until Gannon and family have reviewed and approved the exact output.
