# Canva / Adobe Express Instructions

## Canvas

- Primary: 1080 x 1350.
- Secondary: 1080 x 1920.
- Keep all important text inside a 90 px safety margin.

## Layers

1. Near-black background.
2. `luxury-gold-background.jpg` at 25-35% opacity, cropped to remove any watermark.
3. Soft black vignette.
4. Verified hoodie cutout.
5. Verified journal bundle image.
6. Verified mug cutout.
7. Official cover art at 12-18% of canvas width.
8. Thin gold divider.
9. Headline and product prices.
10. CTA.

## Type

- Headline: modern geometric sans-serif, uppercase, medium weight.
- Supporting text: clean sans-serif.
- No script font except an approved Gannon signature asset.
- Letter spacing: modest and readable.

## Colour

- Background: `#080808`.
- Warm gold: `#D4AF37`.
- Soft champagne: `#E8D6A5`.
- Main text: `#F7F4EC`.
- Muted text: `#B8B2A6`.

## Finish

- Add a restrained gold sweep across the headline only.
- Do not place particles over product details.
- Keep prices readable at phone size.
- Export PNG at full resolution.
- Save an editable master before approval.
