# Premium Merch Advertisement Brief

## Objective

Create one premium black-and-gold advertisement that moves viewers from emotional recognition to the Gannon Waye store without feeling like a discount-store graphic.

## Core Idea

**Wear the words that carried you.**

The ad should feel like a music-campaign image first and a store advertisement second. Products appear as physical extensions of the Thankyou and Respect Is Earned story.

## Featured Products

1. Respect Is Earned Hoodie - $89.
2. Respect Is Earned Coffee Mug - $9.90.
3. Thankyou Journal, Pen and Thermos Flask Bundle - $59.
4. Winter Writing & Comfort Bundle - $129. No further discounts apply.
5. Respect Is Earned Wall Poster - assorted sizes; live store currently shows from $39.

Tote bag and CDs may appear only in a small sold-out/social-proof treatment.

## Composition

- Format: 1080 x 1350 primary; adapt to 1080 x 1920 Story.
- Background: near-black with subtle warm gold illumination.
- Hero: hoodie on left or centre, journal bundle on right, mug in foreground.
- Add the official Thankyou cover as a small campaign anchor, not as the largest object.
- Use one thin gold rule, one restrained light sweep and minimal particles.
- Keep at least 15% negative space around the headline.

## Copy Hierarchy

1. `WEAR THE WORDS THAT CARRIED YOU.`
2. `THE THANKYOU COLLECTION`
3. `Hoodie $89 | Mug $9.90 | Journal Bundle $59`
4. `Winter Writing & Comfort Bundle $129`
5. `No further discounts apply to the Winter Bundle.`
6. `SHOP GANNONWAYE.COM/STORE`

## Brand Direction

- Premium, cinematic, restrained and human.
- Gold is an accent, not a flood.
- No discount bursts, starbursts, crowded stickers or fake scarcity.
- Type should be modern uppercase sans-serif with generous spacing.
- Preserve the official cover typography when the artwork appears.

## CTA

`Shop the collection at gannonwaye.com/store`

## Approval Gate

Before export, confirm exact product images, poster starting price, bundle contents, and that all sold-out labels match the live store.
