# Visual Brief

## Format

- 1080 x 1920 vertical video.
- 15 to 20 seconds.
- Review-only export until approved.

## Visual Direction

Warm, quiet, cinematic, and human. Use the official `Thank You` cover and banner as the safe visual backbone. Avoid family/Sonia imagery and avoid product-price claims.

## Shot Order

1. 0:00-0:03 - Official banner or cover, slow push-in.
2. 0:03-0:07 - On-screen text: `I was not sure if I should ever share this.`
3. 0:07-0:12 - Cover detail or simple gold-light movement.
4. 0:12-0:16 - On-screen text: `A song for the ones who stayed.`
5. 0:16-0:20 - CTA card: `Who are you saying thankyou to today?`

## Edit Notes

- Keep movement slow and restrained.
- Do not use fake fans, fake family images, or generated portraits.
- Use readable captions with high contrast.
- No live store claims, prices, limited-stock urgency, or scheduling language.
