## Composition

- Use only the real product images in `../00-assets-inbox/`.
- Keep product scale believable and preserve all garment artwork.
- Use one hoodie view, one mug view and one bundle composition as the primary hierarchy.
- Tote imagery may appear only with a clear `SOLD OUT` treatment.
- Leave breathing room around products; avoid collage clutter.
- Use warm amber rim light and subtle gold accents, not bright yellow.

## Copy

- Use the approved headline and CTA from `merch-ad-copy.md`.
- Store link: `gannonwaye.com/store`.
- Winter bundle copy must state `$129` and `No further discounts apply`.
- Do not state a numeric poster price until final size pricing is approved.

## Required Exports

- 1080 x 1350 feed.
- 1080 x 1920 Story/Reel cover.
- 1080 x 1080 square.
- 1920 x 1080 landscape.
- Editable master.

Do not publish directly from Canva or Adobe Express.
