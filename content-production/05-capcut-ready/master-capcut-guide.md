## Shared Project Settings

- Canvas: 1080 x 1920, 9:16.
- Frame rate: 30 fps unless source footage requires matching.
- Target duration: under 30 seconds where the script allows.
- Safe zones: keep essential copy away from the top 180 px and bottom 320 px.
- Captions: sentence case, high contrast, maximum two short lines.
- Transitions: restrained hard cuts, short dissolves and light sweeps only.
- Color: black, charcoal, antique gold and warm amber.
- Audio: use only the excerpt Gannon approves.
- CTA: `gannonwaye.com/store`.
