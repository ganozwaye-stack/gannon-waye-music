# Google Drive Intake Rules

Drive source of truth:

`G:\My Drive\Gannon Waye Music\Content Production`

## Folder Flow

1. `00-Recordings Inbox` - save raw phone videos, screen recordings, voice notes and unedited takes here.
2. `01-Raw Video To Process` - move usable takes here after first review.
3. `02-Scripts Captions Hooks` - scripts, hooks, captions, first comments and hashtag drafts.
4. `03-Edited Drafts` - CapCut, HeyGen, Canva or editor exports that are not yet approved.
5. `04-Approval Queue` - final draft package waiting for Gannon approval.
6. `05-Approved To Schedule` - approved media, caption, hashtags, link and posting notes.
7. `06-Scheduled In Metricool` - scheduled items awaiting live confirmation.
8. `07-Posted Archive` - final posted media, final copy, public URL and screenshot proof.
9. `10-Performance Reports` - weekly click, sales, subscriber and engagement review.

## Naming

Use:

`YYYY-MM-DD_campaign_platform_preset_version.ext`

Example:

`2026-07-19_thankyou-merch_tiktok_black-gold-v01.mp4`

## Do Not

- Do not auto-post.
- Do not schedule unless an ApprovalQueue item is approved.
- Do not use private memorial content for sales unless the draft explicitly says Gannon approved it.
- Do not delete raw source files until the posted archive and Drive copy are verified.
