# Production Approval Checklist

No asset may move into `08-approved` until every relevant item is checked.

## Accuracy

- [ ] Product names match the live store.
- [ ] Product prices match the live store.
- [ ] Sold-out products are clearly labelled.
- [ ] Bundle contents are exact.
- [ ] Winter Bundle says no further discounts apply.
- [ ] Thankyou is described as out now.

## Brand

- [ ] Black/gold treatment feels premium, not template-driven.
- [ ] Official cover remains undistorted.
- [ ] Gannon's face and products are not altered misleadingly.
- [ ] Text is readable on a phone.
- [ ] Effects do not obscure products or claims.

## Rights and Safety

- [ ] Music excerpt is approved for the intended platform.
- [ ] Every image is owned, licensed or approved.
- [ ] AI-generated imagery is not presented as documentary truth.
- [ ] No private person appears without approval.
- [ ] No outdated social handle appears.

## Publishing

- [ ] Gannon approved the final export.
- [ ] Caption approved.
- [ ] First comment approved.
- [ ] Post date/time approved.
- [ ] Metricool item remains draft until approval.
