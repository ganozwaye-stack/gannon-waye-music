# Brand Editing Presets

Use these presets for every short-form draft so the edits stay recognisable and do not drift from video to video.

## Raw Truth

- Best for: talking-head grief, resilience, behind-the-song, personal updates, coaching drafts.
- Filter: natural skin, mild warmth, contrast +8, saturation -4, grain 8-12%.
- Cuts: keep human pauses; remove only dead air, repeated lines and false starts.
- Text: cream/gold captions, sentence case, two lines max.
- CTA: soft invitation, never hard sell.

## Black Gold Merch

- Best for: THANKYOU merch, product demos, bundle offers and store CTAs.
- Filter: warm black/gold contrast, clean highlights, sharp product detail.
- Cuts: product reveal every 1.5-2.5 seconds; beat-synced when music supports it.
- Text: bold gold product names, price card only after current pricing is confirmed.
- CTA: shop, bundle, limited stock or email capture.

## Memorial Garden

- Best for: Without You Here, Mum's Garden and Sonia memorial material.
- Filter: soft garden warmth, gentle contrast, no heavy funeral look.
- Cuts: slow, respectful, no aggressive zooms or novelty effects.
- Text: minimal lyric-led captions.
- CTA: remembrance, listen, leave a memory. Not merch-first.

## Release Energy

- Best for: TikTok, Reels, Shorts, hooks, fan engagement and new release momentum.
- Filter: clean bright contrast with subtle gold lift.
- Cuts: hook in the first 1-2 seconds, pattern interrupt before second 5.
- Text: strong hook first, one idea per screen.
- CTA: listen, comment, share, join email list or visit store.

## Approval Rule

These presets can be applied automatically in briefs and edit instructions. Final posting still requires Gannon approval.
