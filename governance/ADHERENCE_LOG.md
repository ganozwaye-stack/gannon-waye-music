## Class definitions

**GREEN** means in scope, determinate, no cost, no paid credits, reasonably reversible, and no protected action.

**RED** means spending, paid credits, publishing, external sending, customer contact, pricing, refunds, transfers, merging, deploying, deletion, settings, accounts, credentials, permissions, security controls, contracts, licences, terms, rights, or private information exposure.

Uncertainty is RED.
