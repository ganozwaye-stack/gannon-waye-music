# Owner Briefing

## Issue

What was wrong.

## Evidence

The exact logs, tests, records, file paths, URLs, IDs, or receipts that prove the finding.

## Action taken

What the agent actually changed or prepared.

## Current status

What works now and what was verified.

## Solution

Why the action resolves the issue.

## Remaining blockers

Only unresolved items and the evidence needed to close them.

## Approval required

The exact RED action and target, or `None`.

## Confidence

State confidence and name any gap that could change the conclusion.
