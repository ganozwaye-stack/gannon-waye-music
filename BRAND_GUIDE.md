# Gannon Waye Music Brand Guide

## Emotional centre

Gannon Waye Music is about healing, survival, grief, independence, moral compass, and the love of God without forcing religion onto people. It should help listeners feel less alone and more able to claim strength in their own life.

## Visual direction

- Peaceful, natural, cinematic, Australian, garden-based where appropriate.
- Deep green, warm gold, blue sky, soft cloud light, candle warmth, cream typography.
- Premium but not corporate.
- Emotional but not morbid.
- Spiritual but not scary or heavy.

## Typography mood

- Display headings: elegant serif, emotional, spacious.
- Body copy: readable, calm, plain-spoken.
- Labels: small uppercase, warm gold, used sparingly.

## Do

- Use exact real photos where personal history matters.
- Use Gannon’s approved artwork for music sections.
- Lead with story, song, and healing.
- Keep merch secondary when grief or family memory is the main context.

## Do not

- Use fake people in family photos.
- Use grave/coffin/funeral-room imagery as public page visuals.
- Say “Without You Here” was written in a garden.
- Make Sonia speak new generated words as if she is actually speaking.
- Turn memorial copy into generic grief quotes.
